Host: LBRCE Engineering College

Focus: Advanced 6G, Emerging AI Applications & Coding

TRACK A

Poster Competition

Theme: 6G Technologies for Next-Generation Engineering

Competition Themes
  • 1 6G for Smart Power Systems, Renewable Energy, and Electric Vehicles (EEE)
  • 2 6G Communications, AI-Native Networks, ISAC, and Intelligent Electronics (ECE)
  • 3 6G-Enabled Smart Cities, Intelligent Infrastructure, and Disaster Resilience (Civil)
  • 4 Industry 5.0, Digital Twins, Robotics, and Smart Manufacturing with 6G (Mechanical)
  • 5 AI, Generative AI, Cybersecurity, Edge Computing, and Intelligent Software Systems for 6G (CSE, IT, AI, DS, ML, Cyber Security)
📝 Important Guidelines
  • • Poster Size: A3 | Mode: Printed or Handmade.
  • • Open to students from all departments.
  • • Based on your own innovative idea or the given themes.
  • • No template. Open choice to design creatively.
  • • Individual participation only (No team entries).
  • • Presentation (Pitch): 5–7 minutes + Q&A.
  • • Explain the concept, innovation, applications, and significance.
  • • Certificate Policy: Only the registered participant receives the certificate.
  • • Evaluated on originality, creativity, technical content, clarity, and presentation.
TRACK B

Ideation / Design Competition

Theme: Design Future 6G Solutions

Competition Themes
  • 1 Smart Energy Management and EV Charging Infrastructure using 6G & AI (EEE)
  • 2 Bio Medical, Next-Generation Wireless, IoT, FPGA and Autonomous Communication Systems (ECE)
  • 3 AI Based Smart City, Intelligent Transportation, and Infrastructure Monitoring (Civil)
  • 4 Digital Twin-Based Smart Factory and Intelligent Robotics Solutions (Mechanical)
  • 5 Secure AI, Edge Intelligence, and 6G Applications for Smart Society (CSE, IT, AI, Cyber Security)
🔻 Note
  • 1. Present an innovative idea using a PowerPoint presentation OR a working prototype along with a PowerPoint presentation.
  • 2. PowerPoint presentation is mandatory for all teams, including those presenting a prototype.
  • 3. The presentation should contain 10–15 slides only.
  • 4. Presentation Duration: 5 to 7 minutes (followed by a Q&A session).
TRACK C

Coding Competition

Multi-stage Logic & Algorithmic Challenge

Round 1: Prompt Engineering Challenge

Participants will be provided with AI-generated outputs such as images, videos, websites or other digital content. Their task is to analyze the given output and write the most accurate and detailed prompt that could recreate the given output. Only the written prompt will be evaluated; participants are not required to generate the output using any AI tool.

Round 2: Coding Challenge

Participants shortlisted from Round 1 will advance to the Coding Challenge. Four coding problems (Easy to Medium level) will be provided, and participants may solve them using any programming language (C, C++, Java, Python, etc.).

📝 Important Guidelines
  • • Individual participation only (No team entries).
  • • Round 1 will be evaluated based on prompt quality, clarity, structure, and attention to detail; not on Output.
  • • Only shortlisted participants will qualify for Round 2.
  • • Round 2 consists of four Easy to Medium coding problems.
  • • Participants are free to use any programming language.
  • • Evaluation will be based on correctness, logic, efficiency, and completion within the allotted time.
